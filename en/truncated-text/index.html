Truncated text

Truncated text cuts a long text to a few lines with an ellipsis, and only when it doesn't fit, lets the user read the rest: in a tooltip, or with a "View more" toggle. It comes from Carbon for IBM Products. Use it for descriptions and notes in cards, tables and lists, where a long text would push everything else down.

HTMLReactReact + Tailwind<div style="inline-size: 320px; max-inline-size: 100%"> <ui-truncated-text value="Supply of A4 paper, toner and stationery for the head office and the three regional branches, delivered monthly from January to December 2026, with a price review after six months."></ui-truncated-text> </div> <UiTruncatedText value="Supply of A4 paper, toner and stationery for the head office and the three regional branches, …" /> <TruncatedText value="Supply of A4 paper, toner and stationery for the head office and the three regional branches, …" />

Use it

HTMLReactReact + Tailwind<script type="module">import '@purpurabog/ui/truncated-text';</script> <ui-truncated-text value="Supply of A4 paper, toner and stationery for the head office …"></ui-truncated-text> import { UiTruncatedText } from '@purpurabog/ui/react'; <UiTruncatedText value="Supply of A4 paper, toner and stationery for the head office …" /> import { TruncatedText } from '@/components/ui/react-tw/truncated-text'; <TruncatedText value="Supply of A4 paper, toner and stationery for the head office …" />

The text goes in value, not in the element's content. The element fills its container's width, so the container decides where the lines break.

Full text in a tooltip

type="tooltip" is the default. When the text is cut, it becomes a button that shows the whole text in a tooltip on hover and keyboard focus. Use it where there is no room for a toggle, such as table cells.

HTMLReactReact + Tailwind<div style="inline-size: 240px; max-inline-size: 100%"> <ui-truncated-text lines="1" value="CT-2026-0142: Office supplies for the head office and three regional branches"></ui-truncated-text> </div> <div style={{ inlineSize: 240 }}> <UiTruncatedText lines={1} value="CT-2026-0142: Office supplies for the head office and three regional branches" /> </div> <div className="w-60"> <TruncatedText lines={1} value="CT-2026-0142: Office supplies for the head office and three regional branches" /> </div>

View more

type="expand" adds a "View more" toggle under a cut text. It opens the rest with a short height animation (none with reduced motion), and becomes "View less". Use it for longer notes that people read in full.

HTMLReactReact + Tailwind<div style="inline-size: 360px; max-inline-size: 100%"> <ui-truncated-text type="expand" lines="3" value="Finance asks for two changes before signing: the payment term should be 30 days after delivery instead of 15, and the price review after six months needs a cap of 5%. Legal has no further comments. Once the vendor accepts both changes, send the contract to the department head for the final approval, then to the vendor for signature."></ui-truncated-text> </div> <UiTruncatedText type="expand" lines={3} value={reviewNote} /> <TruncatedText type="expand" lines={3} value={reviewNote} />

Only when it doesn't fit

The element measures the text. A text that fits shows as it is, with no ellipsis, no tooltip button and no toggle; it measures again when the box resizes or value changes.

The payment term should be 30 days after delivery instead of 15, and the price review after six months needs a cap of 5%."></ui-truncated-text> </div> <UiTruncatedText type="expand" value="Reviewed by finance. No changes." /> <UiTruncatedText type="expand" value="Reviewed by finance. The payment term should be 30 days after delivery instead of 15, …" /> <TruncatedText type="expand" value="Reviewed by finance. Lines

lines is how many lines show before the ellipsis: 2 by default. 0 shows the whole text.

HTMLReactReact + Tailwind<div style="inline-size: 320px; max-inline-size: 100%; display: grid; gap: 16px"> <ui-truncated-text lines="1" value="Supply of A4 paper, toner and stationery for the head office and the three regional branches, delivered monthly from January to December 2026."></ui-truncated-text> <ui-truncated-text lines="3" value="Supply of A4 paper, toner and stationery for the head office and the three regional branches, delivered monthly from January to December 2026, with a price review after six months and a cap of 5% on any increase."></ui-truncated-text> <ui-truncated-text lines="0" value="Supply of A4 paper, toner and stationery for the head office and the three regional branches, delivered monthly from January to December 2026."></ui-truncated-text> </div> <UiTruncatedText lines={1} value={scope} /> <UiTruncatedText lines={3} value={scope} /> <UiTruncatedText lines={0} value={scope} /> <TruncatedText lines={1} value={scope} /> <TruncatedText lines={3} value={scope} /> <TruncatedText lines={0} value={scope} />

Toggle words

expand-label and collapse-label (expandLabel, collapseLabel) replace "View more" and "View less" (which follow the page's language). Keep them short and say what opens.

Clause 9 (termination) is fine as written. Please send the revised draft by Friday."></ui-truncated-text> </div> <UiTruncatedText type="expand" expandLabel="Read the full note" collapseLabel="Hide the note" value={legalNote} /> <TruncatedText type="expand" expandLabel="Read the full note" collapseLabel="Hide the note" value={legalNote} />

Tooltip side

placement sets the tooltip's side: top (the default), bottom, left, right, each with -start or -end. align is Carbon's name for it.

(head office)"></ui-truncated-text> </div> <UiTruncatedText lines={1} placement="bottom-start" value="Vendor: Siam Office Supplies and Printing Co., Ltd. Open from code

expanded shows an expandable text in full; set it to open or close several texts at once. Set from code, it changes without the animation. ui-toggle fires with detail.expanded when the user presses the toggle. In React + Tailwind, hold it with expanded and onExpandedChange, or start with defaultExpanded.

It follows its box

The text is measured again whenever its box resizes, so the ellipsis, the tooltip button and the toggle come and go with the layout. Drag the corner of the box below to see it.

Style a cut text

While the text doesn't fit its lines, the element has data-overflowing (kept while it is open). Style on it, for example to mark notes that have more to read. The text itself is the content part. In React + Tailwind the attribute is on the outer <div>, so use data-overflowing: classes.

It takes the font around it

The text has no font of its own: it uses the font, size and colour of where it sits. Put it inside a heading, a caption or a table cell, and it looks like them.

Keyboard

Key Does
Tab Moves to a cut tooltip text (its tooltip shows) or to the toggle
Enter, Space On the toggle: opens or closes the text
Esc Hides the tooltip

Accessibility

  • A cut tooltip text is a button named by the whole text, so screen readers read all of it, not the cut part.
  • The toggle has aria-expanded and aria-controls pointing at the text.
  • A text that fits is plain text: no button, nothing extra to tab through.
  • Don't cut text people must read to finish a task (an error, a legal condition they accept). Show it in full.

Unstyled React parts with the same behaviour: Headless Truncated text.

API

Generated from the package. In React, pass a property as a prop of the same name; events are props too (the React column).

<ui-truncated-text> · React UiTruncatedText

Attributes

Attribute Property Type Default Description
align Carbon's name for placement (the tooltip's side)
value value string '' The text.
lines lines number 2 Lines shown before the ellipsis; 0 = all.
type type TruncatedTextType 'tooltip' What a truncated text offers: the whole text in a tooltip, or a toggle that expands it.
placement placement Placement 'top' The tooltip's side.
expand-label expandLabel string '' The expand toggle's text (default "View more", translated).
collapse-label collapseLabel string '' The collapse toggle's text (default "View less", translated).
expanded expanded boolean false Whether an expandable text shows in full.

Events

Event React Description
ui-toggle onUiToggle detail: { expanded } when the toggle opens or closes the text

CSS parts

Part Description
content The text
trigger The button around the text of a truncated type="tooltip"
toggle The expand / collapse button

CSS custom properties

Custom property Default Description
--ui-focus-offset outline-offset of its focus ring (default -2px: inside; Kanok: 2px outside)
